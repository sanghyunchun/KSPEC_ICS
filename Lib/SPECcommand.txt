[Command]       [Usage: enter in the GUI command field, then press Send]
specstatus      specstatus
specstop        specstop
specinitial     specinitial <night> [experiment]
illuon          illuon
illuoff         illuoff
illulighton     illulighton
illulightoff    illulightoff
getbias         getbias <frames>
getdark         getdark <exposure_seconds> <frames>
getflat         getflat <exposure_seconds> <frames>
getarc          getarc <exposure_seconds> <frames>
getobj          getobj <exposure_seconds> <frames>

Examples
specinitial 20261026
specinitial 20261026 KSPEC_600_430
getbias 10
getdark 600 5
getflat 2.5 3
getarc 3 2
getobj 300 6

<frames> is a positive integer. Exposure time is in seconds and must be finite
and non-negative. Brackets indicate an optional argument; do not type them.

For the IsoPlane server, set SPEC.queue to ISOPLANE in Lib/KSPEC.ini.
Run specinitial before taking frames. The back-illumination stage must be OUT
for frame exposures. illuon moves the stage IN and turns the light on;
illulighton only turns the light on when the stage is already IN.
getflat and getarc do not switch on their lamps. Use flaton/flatoff and
arcon/arcoff separately.
